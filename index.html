About me.

Information theoryDistributed computingCommunication systems

Ali Khalesi is an Assistant Professor at IPSA on a permanent contract (CDI) in Ivry-sur-Seine, France, where I am a member of the Signals and AI group. I am also an invited researcher at LINCS Lab. My current research focuses mainly on satellite communications and drone swarm orchestration, alongside broader interests in information theory, coding theory, distributed computing, and communication systems.

I received my PhD from Sorbonne University in July 2024, carrying out my research at EURECOM under the supervision of Prof. Petros Elia. My thesis, Multi-User Linearly-Decomposable Distributed Computing, investigates the fundamental computation–communication trade-offs in multi-user systems.

Research Interests

  • Fundamental limits of distributed computing and learning
  • Information theory, coding theory, and index coding
  • High-performance computing algorithms
  • Privacy, secret sharing, and distributed cryptographic protocols
  • Decentralized wireless systems and communication networks
